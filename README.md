# 👋 Welcome to JSP-IT Solutions

**Software Development · AI · Automation · Open-Source Solutions · System Integration**

We build practical software and IT solutions that help businesses and organizations modernize their operations, automate workflows, and maintain control over their technology.

JSP-IT Solutions Oy is a Finland-based software and IT services company combining software engineering, open-source technologies, AI, and technical expertise.

**Our goal: useful software, sustainable technology, and solutions that solve real-world problems.**

[🌐 Website](https://jsp-it.com) · [📦 JSP-Product™ Solutions](https://jsp-it.com/products_jsp_overview) · [🤝 Development Group](https://github.com/JSP-IT-Solutions-Group)

## 🚀 What We Do

- **Custom Software Development** — Desktop, web, mobile, and business applications.
- **AI & Intelligent Automation** — AI assistants, agents, workflow automation, and integration.
- **Open-Source-Based Solutions** — Practical software solutions built on established open-source technologies.
- **System Integration** — Connecting applications, services, databases, and business systems.
- **Cloud & Infrastructure** — Deployment, configuration, management, and technical consulting.
- **Security & Reliability** — Secure development, maintainability, testing, and operational continuity.

## 🌍 Open Source & Contributions

Open-source software is an important part of our development approach.

Our contribution interests include:

- Bug fixes and compatibility improvements
- Security and reliability enhancements
- New features and integrations
- Documentation and automated testing
- Developer productivity and DevOps improvements
- AI-assisted development workflows

We aim to contribute improvements upstream where appropriate and follow each project's contribution guidelines and licensing requirements.
